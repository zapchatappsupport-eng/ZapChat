Content & Copyright Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy sets out what you may and may not build with HTML to APK. It is not a complete list. You must also follow the rules of every store and platform where you share your app.

Not allowed

  • Malware, spyware, phishing, or any app that harms devices or steals data.
  • Collecting personal data without clear notice and consent, or hiding what an app does.
  • Apps that impersonate a person, brand, organization or another app, or that mislead users about their purpose.
  • Content that infringes copyright, trademarks or other rights, including copying another app's design or content without permission.
  • Any sexual content involving minors, content that exploits or endangers children, or content that promotes violence, hate or harassment.
  • Illegal content or activity, including illegal gambling, fraud and scams.
  • Mass-producing many near-identical apps to spam a store.

Reporting misuse

We cannot see the apps people build, because everything is processed on the builder's device, and we do not host them. If you find an app that was made with HTML to APK and breaks this policy, please also report it to the store that hosts it. You can email us at jyotirmoydas020@gmail.com and we will review the report. We may remove access to the Service for people who misuse it.

Copyright complaints

If you believe your work is being used without permission, email jyotirmoydas020@gmail.com with: a description of the work; where you found the infringing app or file; your name and contact details; a statement that you are acting in good faith; and a statement that the information is accurate and that you own or represent the owner of the rights.
