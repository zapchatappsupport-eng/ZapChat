Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how HTML to APK (the "App" and this website, together the "Service"), operated by Jyobharat ("we", "us"), handles information.

In short: we do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

1. Your files stay on your device

When you choose an HTML file, a logo, a splash screen file or a background image or video, the App reads it through Android's file picker and processes it on your device to build your APK. These files are not uploaded to us or to any server.

The APK you create is saved in the App's private storage. It is copied to your Downloads folder only when you tap Download, and shared only when you tap Share, with the app you choose. We do not control what other apps do with a file you share.

2. Signing key

On your first build the App creates a signing key to sign your APKs. It is stored in the App's private storage on your device and never leaves it. If you uninstall the App the key is deleted, and apps you built earlier can no longer be updated with the same key.

3. Permissions

The App does not request any Android permissions and does not connect to the internet.

4. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (for example IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to deliver and secure the site. We do not use these logs to identify you.

5. Apps you create

The apps you create are yours. They show the HTML you provide and may use the Internet permission to load web content. We do not add ads, analytics or trackers to them. If your HTML collects data, uses third-party services or shows ads, you are responsible for your own privacy policy and for the disclosures required by the stores where you publish (for example Google Play's Data safety form).

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or request about your privacy, contact us at jyotirmoydas020@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
